ABOUT THIS SITE

Independent PRINCE2 learning, without the noise.

PRINCE2 Guide is an independent educational publication built to make project-management concepts easier to read, understand and revisit.

WHY IT EXISTS

Clarity first.

The site is designed around a simple idea: useful project-management knowledge should be easy to scan when you are studying and comfortable to read when you are going deep.

EDITORIAL APPROACH

Practical over noisy.

Expect explainers, study notes, terminology breakdowns, comparisons and practical examples, presented in a calm reading environment.

Important: This is an independent learning resource. It is not an official PRINCE2 or PeopleCert website.
