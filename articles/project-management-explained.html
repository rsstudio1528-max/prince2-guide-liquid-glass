PRINCE2 Project Management Explained

A plain-English introduction to how a structured project management method can turn complex work into manageable decisions.

By Alex Morgan·September 2026·7 min read
PRINCE2
PROJECT MANAGEMENT • LEARNING • PRACTICE

Project management is the discipline of coordinating people, plans, decisions, money, time and deliverables so that a project can move toward a defined outcome.

Why structure helps

Projects become difficult when responsibilities are unclear or decisions arrive too late. A structured method creates places where the team can stop, review and decide.

Manage by information

Useful controls depend on information. Teams need a shared understanding of progress, risks, issues, quality and the reason the project still matters.

Keep the method proportional

Good governance should fit the project. A small project does not need the same overhead as a large, complex initiative.

In practice

A simple weekly rhythm—review progress, look ahead, surface risks and issues, confirm decisions—can make project management feel much less reactive.
