What Does the PRINCE2 Exam Involve?

An orientation guide to approaching PRINCE2 assessment preparation without relying on memorisation alone.

By Alex Morgan·September 2026·6 min read
PRINCE2
PROJECT MANAGEMENT • LEARNING • PRACTICE

Exam preparation is easier when you understand the style of learning being assessed. Start from your current syllabus or training material and build familiarity with the terminology before drilling questions.

Read the question precisely

Separate the project facts from the wording that describes the management situation. Look for the specific concept being tested.

Do not over-interpret

Use the scenario that is actually presented. Avoid inventing facts that are not there.

Review mistakes

After each practice question, explain why your chosen answer seemed attractive and what clue you missed. That review is often more valuable than simply recording a score.
