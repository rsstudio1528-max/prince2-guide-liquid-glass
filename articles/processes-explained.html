PRINCE2 Processes Explained

Understand the flow of PRINCE2 processes and how each process supports decision-making through the life of a project.

By Alex Morgan·September 2026·9 min read
PRINCE2
PROJECT MANAGEMENT • LEARNING • PRACTICE

Processes give PRINCE2 a flow. They help people understand which management activity belongs where in the life of a project.

Starting up a project

This process establishes whether the project is sufficiently worthwhile and ready to be initiated.

Directing a project

Direction sits with the project board and concerns the major decisions needed to maintain project control.

Initiating a project

Initiation turns the outline into a robust management foundation for the project.

Managing a stage boundary

At a stage boundary, the project looks ahead, reviews performance and prepares the information needed for the next decision.

Managing product delivery

This process connects delivery work with the agreements made between the project manager and teams producing products.

Closing a project

Closing brings the project to an orderly finish, including confirming completion and capturing lessons.

Key concept

Processes are easier to remember when you understand their purpose in the project lifecycle rather than treating the names as an isolated list.
