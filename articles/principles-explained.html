PRINCE2 Principles Explained

A practical guide to the principles and the role they play in keeping PRINCE2 consistent across different project environments.

By Alex Morgan·September 2026·9 min read
PRINCE2
PROJECT MANAGEMENT • LEARNING • PRACTICE

PRINCE2 principles are the enduring ideas that guide how the method should be used. They help the method stay coherent even when the project itself changes.

Why principles matter

Without principles, a method can become a collection of templates. Principles give teams a reason behind the management behaviours they are expected to use.

Use principles as questions

Instead of memorising a list only as labels, turn each principle into a question you can ask about a project.

  • Is there still a clear reason for the project?
  • What can the team learn from previous work?
  • Are responsibilities and decision rights clear?
  • Has the approach been tailored to fit the project?

Tailoring

Tailoring is especially useful when thinking about real projects. The method is intended to be adapted to the project's scale, risk, complexity and context.

Important

Tailoring does not mean removing the logic of the method. It means adapting how the method is applied while retaining effective governance.

Study approach

Write a one-sentence explanation for each principle and then create a simple project example that demonstrates it.
