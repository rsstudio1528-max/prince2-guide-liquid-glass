PRINCE2 Practices Explained

A readable guide to the management areas covered by PRINCE2 practices and how they connect to day-to-day project control.

By Alex Morgan·September 2026·10 min read
PRINCE2
PROJECT MANAGEMENT • LEARNING • PRACTICE

PRINCE2 practices describe the management areas that need attention throughout a project. They provide a useful lens for understanding what the project manager and wider project team are continuously managing.

Business case

This practice keeps attention on why the project is worth doing and whether that justification remains valid.

Organizing

Organizing clarifies who is accountable, who makes decisions and how the project management team is structured.

Plans

Plans turn intentions into an agreed path of work, including what needs to be delivered and when.

Risk

Risk management is about identifying uncertainty, assessing its effect and agreeing an appropriate response.

Issues

The issues practice provides a structured way to manage matters that need a decision or change to the agreed project path.

Progress

Progress helps the team compare actual performance with what was agreed and decide whether corrective action is required.

In practice

When revising practices, connect each one to an artifact or conversation you would expect to see in a real project.
