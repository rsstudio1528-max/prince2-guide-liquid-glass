PRINCE2 and Agile: Understanding the Difference

A neutral look at how structured project governance and iterative delivery ideas differ, and where the concepts can be used together.

By Alex Morgan·September 2026·8 min read
PRINCE2
PROJECT MANAGEMENT • LEARNING • PRACTICE

PRINCE2 and Agile are often discussed as if they were competing labels. In practice, they describe different layers of project thinking.

Governance versus delivery style

PRINCE2 is a project management method with a strong focus on governance, decision-making and control. Agile approaches commonly emphasise iterative delivery, feedback and adaptation in the way work is produced.

Why teams combine ideas

A project may use a structured governance approach while delivery teams work iteratively. The important point is to understand which problem each practice is solving.

Important

Do not treat “Agile” as one single method. Compare specific practices and contexts rather than labels alone.

A useful mental model

Think about governance, planning and delivery as connected but distinct questions. That helps avoid false either/or comparisons.
